Participant Experience Questionnaire

Helping us improve the research participant experience

Thank you for taking part today. Your feedback in this questionnaire will help us design future studies for people with lived experiences of mental health conditions. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can skip any question you would prefer not to answer. The form should take around 5–10 minutes.

Participant Information

Section A. Your understanding of brain stimulation
1. Before joining this study, what was your understanding of brain stimulation or neuromodulation?
2. Has taking part today changed your understanding of what brain stimulation involves?
3. How much detail would someone want to know about how the stimulation works before agreeing to take part?
4. What information about possible side effects would be most important to include? (Tick all that apply.)
Section B. How this compares with other treatment options
5. If you were offered a choice for a mental health condition, would you prefer medication or brain stimulation of the kind used in this study?
6. What do you see as the main advantages of this type of brain stimulation, compared with other treatments?
7. What do you see as the main drawbacks or concerns?
Section C. The practical experience of taking part
8. How did you find the overall length of today's session?
9. How many sessions like today's would feel acceptable to you in total?
10. Was anything about the practical arrangements (travel, timings, the building, the team, what we asked you to do) particularly easy or particularly difficult?
Section D. Designing future research

Your perspective on how to make research participation work well for different groups of people is very valuable.

11. If you were recommending this study to someone with a mental health condition, what would you tell them was the best part of taking part?
12. And what would you tell them was the hardest part?
13. Is there anything we could have done differently today to make the session more comfortable, clearer, or easier for someone who is unwell?
14. What information or support do you think someone with a mental health condition would most want before deciding to take part?
15. Is there anything else you would like us to know that we have not asked about?
