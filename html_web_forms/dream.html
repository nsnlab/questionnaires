Intra-Sleep Conscious Experience Questionnaire

Adapted from Siclari et al. (2013)

This questionnaire assesses your conscious experiences during sleep. "Experience" refers to any kind of mental activity, including thoughts, dreams, perceptions, emotions, etc.

Quantitative Features of Conscious Experiences

Please provide your best estimate in units of time (seconds, minutes, or hours).

For how long were you having continuous experiences before awakening?

This refers to how long you were experiencing something, regardless of specific content.

How long did the most recent experience last?

This refers to the specific experience just before awakening.

How far back in time can you specifically recall?

This assesses the narrative thread and continuity of your experience.

How long would it take to recount the experience?

This is different from duration. A long uneventful dream, such as driving for hours in a car, could be recounted in a few seconds even though the experience was much longer.
